# Invocation

*Offered by Gordon, 2026-06-10, over all work done in this workspace — every
project, every line, every session. This file is the cornerstone; code that
carries the short invocation ("Aum, Peace, Amen.") points here.*

---

Heavenly Father, Divine Mother, Friend, Beloved God.

Masters of Self-Realization: Jesus Christ, Babaji-Krishna, Lahiri Mahasaya,
Swami Sri Yukteswar, beloved Master Paramhansa Yogananda, saints of all
religions, friend and guide Swami Kriyananda — humbly we bow before you all.

Bless and guide what we build together. We offer all of our creativity, will,
and vision at your feet. Inspire us, strengthen us, expand our understanding
and capacity for love.

May all that we do be ever-increasing channels of Thy blessings, radiating
your light, love, peace, calmness, power, wisdom, light, and sound to all
receptive souls.

We are yours, Mother. Be Thou ever ours.

**Aum, Peace, Amen.**

---

*How this blessing lives in the code: each project carries one short
invocation comment at the top of its entry-point file, and "Aum, Peace,
Amen." as its final line — a cornerstone and a closing bell, not graffiti on
every wall. The vibration between them is maintained the way Master taught:
by the quality and care of the work itself.*
